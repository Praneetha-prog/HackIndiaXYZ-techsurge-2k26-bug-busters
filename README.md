# 🇮🇳 SevaSetu

### Smart Government Scheme Discovery & Eligibility Platform

SevaSetu is a web-based platform designed to help citizens discover
government schemes that may be relevant to them based on their personal
details and eligibility requirements.

---

## Problem Statement

Many citizens are unaware of the government schemes and welfare programs
available to them. Even when information is available online, finding the
right scheme and understanding its eligibility criteria can be difficult.

Citizens often have to search through multiple websites and government
portals to find relevant information.

---

##  Our Solution

SevaSetu provides a simple and accessible platform where users can enter
their personal details and discover government schemes that may be
relevant to their profile.

The platform helps users understand scheme eligibility and provides
access to relevant official government portals.

---

## ✨ Key Features

- 🔍 Government scheme discovery
- 🎯 Eligibility-based scheme matching
- 👤 User authentication
- 🎙️ Voice-based input
- 🌐 Multilingual support
- 📄 Required document identification
- ✅ Document verification before application
- 🔗 Direct links to official government scheme portals
- 📱 User-friendly and responsive interface
